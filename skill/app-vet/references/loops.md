# The six loops

A prospect qualifies with one. The best clients stack two or three — that's where 10×+ lives. Score only loops the business **already runs offline**; anything else is a retrofit, not a numerator.

---

## 1. Calendar — something starts at a time

**Customer thought:** "It's Sunday." / "6pm class." / "Practice moved."

The strongest loop, because the business already owns the schedule and push notifications convert it into opens directly. Retention runs ~3× higher for users who receive notifications in their first 90 days.

**Who has it:** churches and ministries · gyms, CrossFit, dance, martial arts · youth sports leagues · private schools, daycare, preschool · nonprofits with volunteer shifts · music and tutoring studios

**F range:** 4–15/mo

---

## 2. Reorder — they buy the same thing again

**Customer thought:** "The usual."

Loyalty is the monetization, not the feature. Enrolled members visit ~20% more often and spend ~20–38% more per ticket. The app doesn't create the visit — it enlarges it. **Gated on scale:** needs 2+ locations or high daily volume to clear the retainer.

**Who has it:** coffee and bakery · fast casual and QSR · barbershops and salons · nail and lash studios · car wash subscriptions · meal prep · pet grooming

**F range:** 2–8/mo

---

## 3. Progress — something accumulates

**Customer thought:** "Where am I in the program?"

A streak, a weight, a chapter, a week of recovery. The record only has value if it lives in one place, and that place becomes the app. Highest emotional switching cost of the six — these clients churn least.

**Who has it:** coaches (fitness, life, business) · physical therapy · tutoring and test prep · med spas and weight-loss clinics · recovery programs · music instruction

**F range:** 4–12/mo

---

## 4. Access — the app is the key

**Customer thought:** "I need it to get in."

Door codes, member cards, ticket scans, gated content. Weak on its own — but it multiplies every other loop, because deleting the app has an immediate physical cost.

**Who has it:** 24h-access gyms · coworking · private clubs · ticketed venues · membership associations · paid communities

**F range:** 1–12/mo (mirrors whatever it gates)

---

## 5. Drop — new things land and fans want them first

**Customer thought:** "Did it come out yet?"

Requires a real release cadence *and* roughly 2,000+ genuinely engaged fans. This is the only loop that qualifies artists, and it disqualifies most of them. A painter selling occasional originals has a website. A touring musician with merch, tickets, and a release schedule has an app.

**Who has it:** touring musicians · gospel and worship artists · creators with paid tiers · podcasters · streetwear and limited-run brands · comedians on a circuit

**F range:** 3–12/mo

---

## 6. Status — an open question with a changing answer

**Customer thought:** "Is it done yet? Where is it?"

Anxiety is the engine. **Diagnostic:** if customers currently call or text to ask, every one of those calls is a capturable app open *and* a labor cost you can bill against. Count the calls — that's F, and the front-desk hourly rate is most of V.

**Who has it:** auto repair · property management · pet boarding and vets · self-storage · home builders · immigration and family law · logistics and moving · staffing shift fill

**F range:** 1–6/mo, but V is unusually high

---

# Retrofit playbook

For prospects scoring 1–3×. The move is never to refuse — it's to find the loop the business *could* be running and sell that first. The retrofit is a new service the client wasn't previously offering, which is what justifies the higher retainer.

| Prospect | Why it fails | Retrofit | Creates | New R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Insurance broker | Quotes once, never returns | Policy vault + renewal countdowns + instant certificate-of-insurance download for trades clients + claim status + referral reward | Status + Calendar | ~4× |
| Oral surgery / dental specialist | One procedure, then gone | Day-by-day post-op recovery protocol: what to eat today, what's normal today, swelling photo upload, escalation button. 14 consecutive opens per patient, cuts post-op call volume, gives the practice something to show referring dentists | Progress + Status | ~5× |
| Roofing / HVAC / plumbing | Emergency only, no rhythm | Sell an annual maintenance membership and make the app the membership — seasonal reminders, filter shipments, photo record of every visit, priority dispatch | Calendar + Status | ~4× |
| Single-location restaurant | Loop is real, scale isn't | Don't retrofit — aggregate. One build sold to 4 non-competing local spots as a shared loyalty network, or wait for location two | Reorder at scale | ~6× |
| Real estate brokerage | Transaction ends | Transaction tracker for active buyers/sellers (docs, milestones, next action) + post-close home-value and maintenance calendar | Status + Calendar | ~4× |
| Financial advisor | Annual review only | Portfolio snapshot + goal progress + document vault + quarterly review cadence | Progress + Status | ~4× |

**Pattern behind all of them:** find the phone calls the business already receives, or the anxiety it already causes, and turn it into a screen. Then find something that recurs on a calendar and attach a notification to it.

---

# The dead zone

No retrofit reliably rescues these. Sell a website and keep the relationship honest.

Wedding photographers and venues · funeral homes · moving companies · transactional law firms · solar installers · general contractors · B2B consultants · visual artists selling originals · one-time medical procedures with no aftercare window

**Common trait:** the customer relationship has a natural end date and no reason to reopen after it.
